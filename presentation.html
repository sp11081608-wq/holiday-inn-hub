Nothing structural changed. Execution changed. That is the most encouraging finding in this entire analysis — it means the fix is operational, and operational fixes are fast and cheap compared to capital ones.
Root cause

The guest data points at three things.

  • Problem handling — score 40.74. When something goes wrong, we do not recover it. This is the #1 broken link and it is a process fix, not a capital one.
  • Shower and bath condition — score 65.98. Our worst-scoring physical area. This is where targeted capital actually earns its return.
  • Three- and four-night stays score worst of any length. Port workers, Metaplant contractors, and Gulfstream engineers are exactly the guests we are disappointing — and exactly the ones who rebook weekly.
  • Breakfast stays paid at ~$15. That is an ownership decision and this plan respects it. The problem is not the price — it is that we never explain the value.

Assets we are not using

We are sitting on advantages we never market.

  • The outdoor fireplace. No competitor in Pooler has one. It appears in none of our OTA photography and is mentioned at zero check-ins. Cost to fix: nothing.
  • 100's Grill food quality. Genuinely better than the free breakfast down the street — including Holiday Inn Express. We compete on food and lose on the word "free."
  • Location. Walking distance to Tanger Outlets, five minutes to SAV, fifteen to Gulfstream, fifteen to twenty to the Hyundai Metaplant.
  • The building itself. Opened 2019 — newer than most of the comp set, with a large meeting room and a strong fitness area.

Seven initiatives. Every one has an owner, a cost, and a KPI.

Fixes the 40.74 problem-handling score. A recovery script, a response standard, and a daily review check. This is the single highest-return item in the plan.

Sequencing

Ninety days, in three phases.

WEEKS 1–2
Foundations & critical fixes
Room-readiness gate before any room is released for sale. Check-in script live. Service-recovery process and review-response standard in place. Cost: effectively zero.
WEEKS 2–4
Experience & value
Bathroom condition audit and targeted repairs. Breakfast value messaging. Fireplace into OTA photography and check-in script. Loyalty recognition standard.
ONGOING
Consistency & measurement
Mid-stay refresh for extended stays. Weekly review monitoring. Re-pull STR at day 90 and measure occupancy index against today's 89.
Phase one is free. That is intentional — it lets us prove the approach works before the hotel commits capital to anything in phase two.
Demand we are not capturing

The rooms exist. So does the demand. We are not connected to it.
