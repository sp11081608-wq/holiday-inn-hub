Root cause

The guests already told us what is wrong.

  • Problem Handling is the lowest score in the file. When a stay goes wrong, we do not save it — and that is the exact moment a guest decides whether to write a 3★ or a 5★.
  • We perform worst on 3–4 night stays — the port, Hyundai, and JCB contractor business we most want to grow.
  • This is fixable without capital. Recovery is a script, a menu of remedies, and a 48-hour response rule.
We held #1–2 in Pooler from 2021 to 2023. Today Drury holds #1 at 4.8★ in our price range, and a new Fairfield is adding reviews about three times faster than we are. This is a recovery, not a first climb — but the window narrows every month.
