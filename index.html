💡 Strategic Insights
Outdoor fireplace is our best untapped asset. No competitor has one. Fix the OTA listing photos and mention it at check-in. Zero cost.
Evening amenity beats free breakfast. Drury's Kickback drives more reviews than breakfast. A free drink at the bar 5–7pm mirrors this and is cheaper.
Guest laundry is a gap. Hampton and Courtyard have it. Port workers on extended stays need it.
Fairfield Marriott is the biggest threat. New, 4.8★, free breakfast, Marriott loyalty. Growing fast.
Courtyard is our real business traveler rival — Starbucks, NFL, projector. Counter with food quality + fireplace + warmth.
